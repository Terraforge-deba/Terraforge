⚙ Settings
Controls: WASD move · Space jump · Shift sprint · E inventory & crafting · M mute
Hold left click to mine · Right click to place (or open a Workbench) · 1–9 / wheel pick slot · Esc pause
Sandbox: F toggles fly (Space up / C down), instant breaking, unlimited blocks.
Expedition: chop acacia logs → timber → workbench → tools. Find coal in the gneiss for glass, mud bricks and lanterns.
⛏ Campaign: hold an exploration tool and use it (right-click / tap / L2). N (or R3, or 📒) opens the field notebook.
⚔ Night: Night Walkers come out in the dark — light lanterns, craft a sword, or craft a gun + bullets at a workbench. Click/R2 on them to hit; right-click/L2 fires the gun. ⛑ health refills slowly.
📱 Phone / tablet: drag the left side to walk (push to the edge to sprint), drag the right side to look · tap the screen to place / use · hold still on the screen (or hold ⛏) to mine · ⤒ jump · 🎒 inventory.
🎮 PS4 controller: Left stick move · Right stick look · ✕ jump · R2 mine · L2 place / use · L1 R1 or D-pad ◀ ▶ hotbar · △ inventory · □ fly (Sandbox) · ○ fly down · L3 sprint · OPTIONS pause. In menus: D-pad or left stick to move, ✕ select, ○ back, □ quick-move an item. Press any button to wake the controller.
🎙 Narrator: V (or SHARE on the controller, or the 🎙 button) starts/stops live commentary while you play.
